About us

The net café started on the 12th of August 2023. The business is by Sarah who has experimented with different types of coffees and lattes or even teas. The business continuously growing the types of drinks they offer.
The target audience is any individual who enjoy a good cup of coffees, lattes and teas. The business also does different types of foods with the freshest and best quality products.
The business mission and vision are to establish a new branch in Cape Town. While improving the quality of the comforting beverages and service provide at the café.

Goals

The company would like to increase the website traffic. This is by providing additional information about the new product online and new product promotion. The customer can also order on the site.
KPIs Using SMART

  1. Increasing the online sales by 20% within 2.5 months
  2. Increasing the website traffic by 30% within 4 months

Future Plans for 2027

The company has several future plans to expand its reach and improve the customer experience. These include opening new branches in other cities and introducing more diverse menu options. The company has already started to open new branches which will open next year (2027) on the 6th of May. On that day the cafe will be doing a giveaway and discount to all customers.
